# Propel by Einstein Labs

**The AI growth team that reads your code.** Propel reads your website and repository, then finds customers, gets you into AI answers and writes posts people share. Nothing goes out without your yes.

- **Get customers:** finds the people who would pay, writes to each one from facts about your product, sorts the replies.
- **Get found:** measures how often ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, Bing and Google's AI answers name you, and fixes what's missing.
- **Get talked about:** drafts posts from what you shipped, ranked against your own best work.

Every claim traces to a source. Every channel starts at "it drafts, you approve".
